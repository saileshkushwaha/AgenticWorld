# Internal Audit Guide - Forge Engineering Lifecycle Framework

## Overview
This guide provides procedures for conducting internal audits within the Forge framework. It covers audit planning, execution, reporting, and follow-up.

## Internal Audit Process

### 1. Audit Planning

#### Audit Types
| Type | Purpose | Frequency |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Financial Audit | Financial accuracy | Annual |
| Operational Audit | Process effectiveness | As needed |
| Compliance Audit | Regulatory compliance | Annual |
| IT Audit | Technology controls | Annual |
| Performance Audit | Efficiency and effectiveness | As needed |

#### Audit Planning Steps
1. Define audit objectives
2. Determine audit scope
3. Identify audit criteria
4. Allocate resources
5. Develop audit timeline
6. Communicate audit plan

### 2. Audit Execution

#### Evidence Gathering Methods
| Method | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Interviews | Structured conversations | Understanding processes |
| Document Review | Examining records | Verifying compliance |
| Observation | Watching operations | Validating practices |
| Sampling | Statistical sampling | Large populations |
| Reperformance | Re-executing controls | Testing effectiveness |

#### Documentation Requirements
- Audit program
- Working papers
- Evidence collected
- Findings documentation
- Audit conclusions

### 3. Audit Reporting

#### Report Structure
1. Executive Summary
2. Audit Objectives and Scope
3. Audit Criteria
4. Findings and Observations
5. Recommendations
6. Management Response
7. Action Plan

#### Finding Severity Classification
| Severity | Definition | Action Required |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| Critical | Immediate risk | Immediate action |
| High | Significant risk | Action within 30 days |
| Medium | Moderate risk | Action within 90 days |
| Low | Minor risk | Action within 180 days |
| Observation | Improvement opportunity | Consider for future |

### 4. Audit Follow-Up

#### Follow-Up Process
1. Track action plan implementation
2. Verify corrective actions
3. Validate effectiveness
4. Close findings
5. Report to management

#### Escalation Criteria
- Overdue action items
- Repeated findings
- Management resistance
- Significant risks

## Audit Standards

### International Standards
- **IIA Standards**: Institute of Internal Auditors
- **ISO 19011**: Audit management systems
- **ISACA IT Audit Framework**: IT audit standards

### Audit Ethics
- Independence and objectivity
- Competence and due professional care
- Confidentiality
- Integrity

## Integration with Other Workflows

| Workflow | Integration Point |
|----------|------------------|
| Risk Assessment | Audit risk management |
| Compliance Audit | Audit compliance |
| Quality Audit | Audit quality processes |
| Process Audit | Audit business processes |
| Data Audit | Audit data quality |
| Configuration Audit | Audit configurations |

## Best Practices
1. Maintain auditor independence
2. Use risk-based audit planning
3. Document all audit evidence
4. Communicate findings clearly
5. Follow up on action plans
6. Maintain professional skepticism
7. Continuously improve audit processes
