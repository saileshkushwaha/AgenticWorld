# ISO 27001 Preparation Capability

## Definition
The ability to prepare organizations for ISO 27001 certification.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can understand ISO 27001 requirements |
| 2 | Competent | Can assist in ISO 27001 preparation activities |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead ISO 27001 preparation initiatives |
| 4 | Expert | Can manage enterprise-wide ISO 27001 programs |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational information security practices |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Scope definition, risk assessment, control selection, policy development, internal audit
**Out of Scope**: Certification audit, physical security assessment

## Skills
- **Standards**: ISO 27001 requirements, Annex A controls, ISO 27002 guidance
- **Risk Assessment**: Asset identification, threat analysis, risk treatment
- **Documentation**: ISMS documentation, policies, procedures
- **Audit**: Internal audit, management review, continual improvement

## Quality Criteria
- Scope defined and approved
- Risk assessment complete
- Controls selected and implemented
- Internal audit passed
