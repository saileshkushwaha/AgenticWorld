# SOC 2 Preparation Capability

## Definition
The ability to prepare organizations for SOC 2 certification.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can understand SOC 2 requirements |
| 2 | Competent | Can assist in SOC 2 preparation activities |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead SOC 2 preparation initiatives |
| 4 | Expert | Can manage enterprise-wide SOC 2 programs |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational trust services practices |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Scope definition, control design, gap assessment, remediation, evidence collection
**Out of Scope**: Audit engagement, subservice organization management

## Skills
- **Standards**: Trust services criteria, COSO framework
- **Control Design**: Control mapping, control descriptions, evidence definition
- **Gap Assessment**: Current state evaluation, gap identification, remediation planning
- **Evidence**: Evidence collection, continuous monitoring, auditor interaction

## Quality Criteria
- Scope defined
- Controls designed and implemented
- Gap assessment complete
- Evidence collection operational
