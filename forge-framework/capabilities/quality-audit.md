# Quality Audit Capability

## Definition
The ability to systematically evaluate quality management systems and processes to ensure standards are met and continuous improvement is achieved.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can assist in quality audits |
| 2 | Competent | Can conduct quality audits |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead quality audit teams |
| 4 | Expert | Can design quality audit programs |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational quality culture |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Quality management systems, process audits, product audits, supplier audits
**Out of Scope**: Product testing, laboratory analysis

## Skills
- **Audit Planning**: Scope definition, criteria selection, checklist development
- **Audit Execution**: Evidence gathering, interviews, observation
- **Nonconformance Identification**: Root cause analysis, corrective action
- **Reporting**: Findings documentation, recommendations

## Quality Criteria
- Audit criteria clearly defined
- Evidence objectively gathered
- Findings accurately documented
- Recommendations actionable and tracked
