# Flow Time Tracking Capability

## Definition
The ability to measure, analyze, and optimize the time work items spend in different workflow states to identify bottlenecks and improve flow efficiency.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can track basic cycle time |
| 2 | Competent | Can implement flow time tracking |
| 3 | Proficient | Can design flow optimization strategies |
| 4 | Expert | Can design enterprise flow measurement systems |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational flow culture |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Cycle time, lead time, queue time, flow efficiency, bottleneck analysis
**Out of Scope**: Individual time tracking, timesheet management

## Skills
- **Time Tracking**: State-based tracking, transition tracking, automated collection
- **Flow Analysis**: Bottleneck identification, queue analysis, flow efficiency calculation
- **Optimization**: WIP limits, process changes, automation
- **Visualization**: Cumulative flow diagrams, cycle time scatterplots

## Quality Criteria
- Workflow states clearly defined
- Time tracking accurate and reliable
- Baseline established
- Optimization recommendations actionable
