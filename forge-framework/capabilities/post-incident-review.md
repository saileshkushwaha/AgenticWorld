# Post-Incident Review Capability

## Definition
The ability to conduct blameless post-incident reviews identifying root causes and driving improvements.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can participate in post-incident reviews |
| 2 | Competent | Can document incident timelines |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead post-incident reviews |
| 4 | Expert | Can facilitate complex multi-team reviews |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational learning practices |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Timeline reconstruction, root cause analysis, impact assessment, action items
**Out of Scope**: Performance management, disciplinary actions

## Skills
- **Analysis**: Timeline reconstruction, root cause analysis (5 Whys, fishbone)
- **Facilitation**: Blameless culture, psychological safety, constructive dialogue
- **Documentation**: Clear writing, actionable recommendations, tracking
- **Follow-up**: Action item tracking, verification, knowledge sharing

## Quality Criteria
- Timeline complete and accurate
- Root cause identified
- Action items created with owners
- Document shared with stakeholders
