# Multi-Cloud Strategy Capability

## Definition
The ability to design, implement, and manage infrastructure across multiple cloud providers to achieve redundancy, cost optimization, and vendor independence.

- Price comparison
- Cost forecasting

### Vendor Management

- SLA monitoring
- Contract management
- Relationship management
- Exit strategy planning

### Operations
- Unified monitoring
- Cross-cloud security
- Compliance management
- Disaster recovery
- Performance optimization
