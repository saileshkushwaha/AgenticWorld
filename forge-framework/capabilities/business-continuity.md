# Business Continuity Planning Capability

## Definition
The ability to develop, implement, and maintain business continuity plans ensuring organizational resilience.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can follow existing BCP procedures |
| 2 | Competent | Can contribute to BCP development and testing |
| 3 | Proficient | Can design BCP for business units |
| 4 | Expert | Can establish enterprise-wide BCP programs |
| 5 | Master | Can create organizational resilience frameworks |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Business impact analysis, risk assessment, recovery strategies, plan documentation, testing
**Out of Scope**: Insurance procurement, financial risk management

## Skills
- **Analysis**: Business impact analysis, risk assessment, dependency mapping
- **Strategy**: Recovery strategies, alternate sites, communication planning
- **Testing**: Tabletop exercises, full-scale drills, scenario planning
- **Maintenance**: Plan updates, lessons learned, continuous improvement

## Quality Criteria
- Business impact analysis complete
- Recovery strategies documented
- Testing program established
- Plan reviewed annually
