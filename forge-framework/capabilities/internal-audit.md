# Internal Audit Capability

## Definition
The ability to conduct independent, objective assurance and consulting operations to improve organizational operations and compliance.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can assist in audit fieldwork |
| 2 | Competent | Can conduct audit engagements |
| 3 | Proficient | Can plan and lead audit engagements |
| 4 | Expert | Can design internal audit programs |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational audit culture |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Internal controls, operational audits, compliance audits, financial audits
**Out of Scope**: External audit representation, legal proceedings

## Skills
- **Audit Planning**: Risk-based audit planning, scoping, resource allocation
- **Audit Execution**: Evidence gathering, testing, analysis
- **Audit Reporting**: Findings documentation, recommendations, action plans
- **Follow-up**: Action plan tracking, remediation verification

## Quality Criteria
- Audit objectives clearly defined
- Evidence sufficient and appropriate
- Findings accurate and actionable
- Recommendations practical and cost-effective
