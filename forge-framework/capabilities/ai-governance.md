# AI Governance Capability

## Definition
The ability to ensure AI systems are developed, deployed, and operated responsibly with appropriate oversight, transparency, and ethical considerations.

## Capabilities

### Governance Framework
- Ethics committee establishment
- Policy development
- Risk assessment
- Compliance monitoring
- Audit procedures

### Bias Management
- Bias detection
- Mitigation strategies
- Fairness metrics
- Continuous monitoring
- Intersectional testing

### Transparency
- Model explainability
- Decision documentation
- Openness about limitations
- User communication
- Audit trail maintenance

### Risk Management
- Risk identification
- Impact assessment
- Mitigation planning
- Incident response
- Continuous improvement
