# Risk Assessment Capability

## Definition
The ability to systematically identify, analyze, and evaluate risks throughout the software development lifecycle.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can identify basic risks using checklists |
| 2 | Competent | Can conduct risk assessments using standard frameworks |
| 3 | Proficient | Can perform quantitative and qualitative risk analysis |
| 4 | Expert | Can design enterprise risk management frameworks |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational risk culture |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Risk identification, risk analysis, risk evaluation, risk treatment, risk monitoring
**Out of Scope**: Financial risk modeling, insurance consulting (consult specialists)

## Skills
- **Risk Identification**: Brainstorming, checklists, SWOT analysis, fault tree analysis
- **Risk Analysis**: Qualitative analysis, quantitative analysis, Monte Carlo simulation
- **Risk Evaluation**: Risk scoring, risk prioritization, risk appetite assessment
- **Risk Treatment**: Risk avoidance, risk mitigation, risk transfer, risk acceptance
- **Risk Monitoring**: Key risk indicators, risk reporting, risk reviews

## Quality Criteria
- Risks systematically identified
- Risk analysis uses appropriate methods
- Risk evaluation is consistent and documented
- Risk treatment plans are actionable
- Risk monitoring is ongoing
