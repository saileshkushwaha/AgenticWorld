# Process Audit Capability

## Definition
The ability to evaluate business processes for efficiency, effectiveness, and compliance with established standards and procedures.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can document existing processes |
| 2 | Competent | Can conduct process audits |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead process improvement initiatives |
| 4 | Expert | Can design process audit frameworks |
| 5 | Master | Can establish process excellence culture |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Process documentation, process compliance, process efficiency, process improvement
**Out of Scope**: Business process reengineering (consult specialists)

## Skills
- **Process Mapping**: Flowcharting, value stream mapping, SIPOC
- **Process Analysis**: Bottleneck analysis, waste identification, cycle time analysis
- **Compliance Assessment**: Policy adherence, procedure compliance, standard conformance
- **Improvement Recommendations**: Process optimization, automation opportunities

## Quality Criteria
- Processes clearly documented
- Audit criteria objectively applied
- Findings supported by evidence
- Recommendations practical and measurable
