# Privacy Capability

## Definition
The ability to ensure privacy compliance and protect personal data throughout the software development lifecycle.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can implement basic privacy notices |
| 2 | Competent | Can manage PII data handling procedures |
| 3 | Proficient | Can conduct privacy impact assessments |
| 4 | Expert | Can ensure regulatory compliance |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational privacy culture |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Data collection consent, privacy rights management, GDPR/CCPA compliance, PII protection
**Out of Scope**: Legal counsel, regulatory reporting (consult legal team)

## Skills
- **Consent Management**: Obtain, track, and manage consent
- **PII Protection**: Identify, classify, and protect PII
- **Privacy Compliance**: Ensure regulatory compliance
- **Data Subject Rights**: Handle access, correction, deletion requests

## Quality Criteria
- Consent properly documented
- PII properly protected
- Compliance requirements met
- Privacy violations prevented
